About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechatronics Engineering at Jyothi Engineering College (JEC). My work spans applied research in computer vision, IoT, and mechatronic product design, with a strong emphasis on solving real problems in agriculture and sustainable engineering. I regularly mentor student innovation projects and contribute to accreditation, faculty development, and industry-collaboration initiatives.
